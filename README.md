# AUSPEX

**A living instrument for watching over the world.**

*The auspex was the one who read the sky for signs and spoke them aloud,*
*so the city could act before the storm. This is that — for the whole planet, for everyone.*

`watchful · honest · open · alive`

</div>

---

AUSPEX is an open, real-time early-warning instrument for the whole planet. Autonomous AI agents
continuously sense the world's signals — disasters, conflict, supply-chain shocks, climate,
outbreaks, mass events — and render them on a living globe that **anyone** can read. The kind of
situational awareness that today lives behind expensive terminals and institutional walls, made
into a commons and given to the world.

```
  what it watches
  ├─ disasters ........ quake · flood · fire · storm
  ├─ conflict ......... unrest · escalation
  ├─ supply chains .... ports · shipping · shortages
  ├─ climate .......... drought · heat · environment
  ├─ public health .... outbreak signals
  └─ human events ..... mass movement at scale

  what it does
  ├─ senses ........... open public signals, geolocated in real time
  ├─ reasons .......... connects events across domains — the link, drawn in light
  ├─ explains ......... plain language, calibrated confidence, sources one tap away
  └─ shows ............ a planet with a pulse: calm where calm, light where not
```

## Principles

- **For everyone.** No login to see the planet. Readable by a child, useful to an analyst, fast on a cheap phone.
- **Honest before impressive.** Uncertainty is shown, not hidden. Sources always one tap away. We never cry wolf.
- **A commons, not a product.** It watches events and systems — never people. A guardian's name, not a spy's.
- **Worthy before public.** It does not ship to the world until it is worthy of everyone in the world.
